BASEBALL QUEST

Privacy notice for parents

Last updated October 2026

Baseball Quest helps a coach assign offseason drills and helps young players keep up with them. Accounts are created by a parent or legal guardian, who then adds their player.

What we collect

  • From the parent: name, email address and password (stored encrypted by our login provider).
  • About the player: first name, last initial and a chosen icon. We don't ask for birthdays, photos, last names or locations.
  • Training activity: which drills were checked off and on what day, and high-fives between teammates.
  • Videos (optional): short drill clips a family chooses to upload.
  • Coach feedback: notes the coach leaves on a player's drills.

Who can see it

  • The player's family sees everything about their own player.
  • The coach sees the players on their teams and their 1-on-1 players, including videos.
  • Teammates' families see a player's first name, last initial, icon, which days they trained, and high-fives. They never see videos or coach notes.
  • 1-on-1 training is visible only to that player's family and the coach.

What we don't do

No ads, no selling or sharing data with marketers, no tracking across other sites, and no use of anyone's data to train AI models. Data is stored with Supabase (our database and login provider) and is used only to run this app.

Your choices

  • Uploading video is always optional.
  • You can remove your player at any time from the ⚙︎ menu on their screen. That permanently deletes their check-offs, XP and videos.
  • To close your whole account or ask what we hold, email the person who invited you to the app (the team's coach or app organizer).

Keeping data only as long as needed

Proof videos are for coach feedback and may be deleted after the season. Training history stays until you remove your player or close your account.
